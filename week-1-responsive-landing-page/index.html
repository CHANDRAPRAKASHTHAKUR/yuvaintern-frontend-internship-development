Features

Everything your team needs, nothing it doesn't

Built around the way modern product teams actually work — plan, execute and learn without switching between six different tools.

  • Kanban boards & sprints

    Drag-and-drop boards, swimlanes and cycle time tracking. Every change is versioned, so you always know who moved what and when.

  • Docs that stay in sync

    Specs, decisions and release notes live next to the work. Mention a teammate with @name and turn any thread into a tracked task instantly.

  • Insightful analytics

    Cycle time, throughput and workload imbalance calculated as you work — no data engineer required to answer "are we getting faster?"

  • Enterprise-grade security

    SSO/SAML, provisioning, granular guest roles and full audit logs. SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.

  • 60+ integrations

    GitHub, GitLab, Figma, Slack, Jira and a REST + webhook API so your workflow never hits a wall.

  • Fast by default

    Optimistic updates, offline-capable caching and a sub-second cold start — because your tools should never be the bottleneck.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Starter plan is free forever and the 14-day Growth trial only asks for a card at the very end, so you can cancel before anything is charged.

Can I migrate from Jira?

Yes — the importer maps projects, issue types, sprints, comments and attachments, and keeps a link back to the original record so nothing is lost.

Is the app accessible?

We target WCAG 2.1 AA, ship full keyboard support and are tested with NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver on every release. An accessibility statement is published with each audit.

Where is my data stored?

In the region you choose at signup (EU, US, India or Australia). Enterprise customers can pin data residency to a dedicated region.
